# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Be respectful. Critique code, not people. Welcome contributors of all backgrounds.

Unacceptable behaviour: harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, and deliberate intimidation.

## Scope

Applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and any community channels.

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported to the maintainer. All reports will be reviewed and handled with discretion. Repeated or severe violations will result in removal from the project.
